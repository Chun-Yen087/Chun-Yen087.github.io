About

Computer Science student. Two co-ops in analytics. Currently at Scotiabank.

I pull data out of business systems, check that it's right, and turn it into dashboards that managers use to make decisions. I've done that work in two very different settings: utility operations at Toronto Hydro and wealth management at Scotiabank.

Co-op experience

Scotiabank · Canadian Wealth Management Analytics
In wealth management, advisors and leaders depend on accurate data about KYC, referrals and financial planning, and that data has to line up across ScotiaMcLeod, Private Banking, Scotiatrust and SJF.

I'm lucky to be on a team whose work goes straight into business decisions. I refresh the Total Wealth Analyzer, the Power BI dashboard branch managers and regional directors use to track their teams, and I'm one of the people users contact when something looks wrong. That support work taught me the most: a dashboard only matters if people trust the numbers in it.

Toronto Hydro · Operations & Data Analytics
Engineering, finance and operations teams used work-order and capital-planning data to schedule work and manage contractors. I cleaned and validated 40,000+ records, built Tableau dashboards and automated weekly contractor scorecards. It was my first look at how much faster a team moves when its data is reliable.

What's next
