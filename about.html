About

Hi, I'm ChunYen. I started in hardware, moved into code, and found my place in data.

I'm a Computer Science co-op student at York University. Right now I'm a Wealth Analytics Intern at Scotiabank, where I help keep a Power BI dashboard running for wealth management teams across Canada.

How I got here

My first studies were in hardware. I did Electrical Engineering at National Taipei University of Technology, then Electronics Engineering at Seneca College, where I programmed Siemens PLCs and worked with embedded systems and robotics.

The part I kept coming back to was the logic behind those systems, so I moved to York for Computer Science. That's where I built my base in programming, databases and algorithms, and started taking data courses.

My first co-op, at Toronto Hydro, is where it clicked. A lot of my time went into cleaning operational data, more than 40,000 records pulled from SAP, SharePoint and Excel, and then building Tableau dashboards and contractor scorecards on top of it. Watching managers use that work in their monthly reporting made me want to keep doing it.

At Scotiabank, I refresh the Total Wealth Analyzer, a Power BI dashboard used by leaders, branch managers and advisors across ScotiaMcLeod, Private Banking and Scotiatrust. I pull Salesforce data with SOQL, process it with Alteryx, Python or SQL depending on the job, and run checks before anything goes out. I'm also one of the people users email when a number looks off. That part has taught me the most: if people don't trust a dashboard, they stop using it.

What I'm looking for

I want to keep building with data, whether that's in analytics, BI, data engineering or software. I've enjoyed working in financial services and would be glad to stay, but what matters most to me is working on problems where the data actually gets used.

I'm also curious about AI agents. A lot of analytics work repeats itself: pulling the same data, running the same checks, answering the same questions. I'd like to build tools that take on those steps. I've started small. My market dashboard uses an LLM to write its summaries, and I built my scheduling app with an AI pair programmer.
